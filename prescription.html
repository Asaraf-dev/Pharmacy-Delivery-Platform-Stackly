Prescription Upload Guidelines

A few simple tips help our pharmacists read your prescription clearly and process your order faster.

Use a Clear Prescription

Make sure the complete prescription is visible, sharp and easy to read.

Patient Details Should Be Visible

Ensure the patient's name and important prescription details can be clearly seen.

Accepted File Formats

Upload your prescription as JPG, PNG or PDF with a maximum size of 10MB.

Use a Valid Prescription

Upload a current and valid prescription issued by a qualified healthcare professional.
